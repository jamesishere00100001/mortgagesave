Introduction

Mortgage Save ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

This policy applies to all users of the Mortgage Save app, regardless of location. We comply with applicable data protection laws including the UK GDPR, EU GDPR, CCPA (California), PIPEDA (Canada), the Australian Privacy Act 1988, and the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Data We Collect

Calculation Data

Mortgage amounts, interest rates, and terms you enter for calculations. This data is stored locally on your device.

Saved Scenarios

When you save mortgage scenarios, this data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Rate Alerts

If you set up rate alerts, we store your alert preferences locally and use push notifications to notify you.

Usage Analytics

With your consent, we send anonymous feature usage and a random app identifier to PostHog Cloud EU to improve the app. We do not send mortgage values, scenario names, notes or contact details. This is optional and can be withdrawn at any time in Privacy Settings.

Purchase Information

If you purchase premium features, the transaction is processed by Apple. We only receive confirmation of purchase status.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide mortgage calculation services
  • To save your scenarios and preferences locally
  • To send rate alert notifications (if enabled)
  • To improve our app through anonymous PostHog Cloud EU analytics (with consent)
  • To provide customer support

Data Storage

Your calculation data and saved scenarios are stored locally on your device using secure iOS storage mechanisms. We do not store your mortgage calculations on our servers.

If you enable iCloud backup on your device, your app data may be included in your iCloud backup, subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Premium purchase status is verified through Apple's StoreKit framework.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Opt out of the sale of your personal information
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

Regional Notices

UK & EU — GDPR Notice

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), you have enhanced rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access (Article 15): You can request a copy of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification (Article 16): You can request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure (Article 17): You can request deletion of your data.
  • Right to Data Portability (Article 20): You can receive your data in a structured format.
  • Right to Object (Article 21): You can object to processing of your data.

To exercise these rights, use the "Export Data" or "Delete Data" options in the app's Settings, or contact us directly.

Legal Basis for Processing:

  • Consent: For analytics and marketing communications
  • Legitimate Interest: For app functionality and improvement
  • Contract: For providing premium services

USA (California) — CCPA Notice

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the following rights:

  • Know what personal information is being collected about you
  • Know whether your personal information is sold or disclosed, and to whom
  • Opt out of the sale of your personal information
  • Access your personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Categories of personal information collected:

  • Identifiers (device ID for analytics, where consented)
  • Commercial information (purchase history)
  • Usage data (app interaction analytics, where consented)

Canada — PIPEDA Notice

Under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), we follow the 10 Fair Information Principles:

  • Accountability: We are responsible for your personal information.
  • Identifying Purposes: We tell you why we collect data.
  • Consent: We obtain your consent for collection and use.
  • Limiting Collection: We only collect what is necessary.
  • Limiting Use, Disclosure, and Retention: We only use data for stated purposes.
  • Accuracy: We keep your information accurate.
  • Safeguards: We protect your information appropriately.
  • Openness: We make our policies available.
  • Individual Access: You can access your information.
  • Challenging Compliance: You can challenge our compliance.

Contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada if you have concerns.
